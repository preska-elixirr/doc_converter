UNION ALPHA / DESIGN 01

A workspace, not a settings maze.

This standalone concept separates operations, the file queue and output settings. Advanced options stay out of the way until you need them.

Try selecting files, switching tools, changing formats, combining documents, adjusting the sample page or running a simulated batch.

Prototype boundaries

No conversion, encryption, cleaning or saving takes place. Added files contribute names and sizes only. Contents are never read. Nothing is uploaded or persisted. Eligibility is illustrative, not the application's Rust capability matrix.

Recipient-key encryption, PDF standards validation, page breaks and merge reordering are outside this concept.
